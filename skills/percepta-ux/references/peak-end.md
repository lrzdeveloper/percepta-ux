# Peak-End: the remembered journey

## Mechanism and boundary

Retrospective judgments can be strongly influenced by emotionally intense moments
and the ending. Negative events can dominate recollection. A journey therefore
needs attention to high-stakes moments and closure as well as its average flow.

This is a context-dependent memory effect, not an arithmetic formula for every
experience and not permission to ignore ordinary frustration. Do not prolong
distress to manufacture a nicer ending or use celebration to conceal a failure.
Measure actual task outcomes and recalled experience separately.

## Diagnose and act

- Define the whole relevant journey, including cross-channel steps and the user's
  real endpoint. “Payment submitted” may not mean “reservation secured.”
- Map actions, questions, expectations, emotional evidence, handoffs, and recovery
  paths. Label predicted feelings as hypotheses. Distinguish the remembered
  peak from your team's favorite animation.
- Repair likely negative peaks first: lost input, unexpected charges, uncertainty
  after submission, blocked access, and unrecoverable mistakes. Preserve work
  and provide a truthful next step and route to help.
- During waits, reduce uncertainty with meaningful state and realistic timing
  information. Operational transparency should reveal useful facts rather than
  imply progress the system cannot measure.
- A useful activity during an unavoidable wait can reduce idle frustration, but
  entertainment should remain optional and appropriate to the task. Honest
  milestones can make proximity to a goal understandable; never manufacture
  completed steps or exploit completion pressure to obtain a new commitment.
- End with clear outcome, receipt or persistent record where appropriate, next
  steps, and any remaining commitment. Celebrate meaningful achievements in a
  tone suited to the situation and with a reduced-motion alternative.
- Humor can be appropriate for a minor dead end; recovery and respectful copy
  matter more when money, access, personal data, or distress is involved.

Use journey mapping from [Research methods](research-methods.md) to locate
uncertainty and opportunities. Biases such as confirmation, negativity, and recency
also affect the team's own interpretation: deliberately inspect disconfirming
observations rather than selecting only memorable anecdotes.

## Verify

Observe completion, error recovery, unresolved cases, and support handoffs. If
the question concerns memory, collect feedback after the relevant interval rather
than treating an immediate satisfaction answer as the entire outcome. Compare
measured behavior and recalled experience; do not invent an emotional curve
without research.

## Original example

A ticket purchase shows a cheerful confirmation while the ticket PDF is still
being generated. Make the confirmed reservation and retrievable reference clear,
show the document's actual state, and offer later retrieval if generation fails.
The ending becomes dependable before it becomes celebratory.
