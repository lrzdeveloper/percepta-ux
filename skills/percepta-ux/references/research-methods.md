## Evidence discipline

Start with the decision and uncertainty. Specify audience/context, neutral tasks,
data, interpretation limits, and what would change the design. Reuse supplied
research. Proposed studies and imagined users are not executed evidence.

Recruit for relevant behavior and access needs; identify missing groups. Audience
cutoffs and sample sizes are local choices to justify, not universal rules.
A small formative pilot exposes problems but cannot establish precise group rates
or commercial uplift. Agree decision thresholds from baseline, task risk and goals,
or mark them as assumptions. Patterns suggest candidate causes, not proven causes.

Obtain appropriate consent, minimize data, and respect actual confidentiality
arrangements. Match method and effort to uncertainty and consequence.

## Classification and findability

**Card sorting** explores names and grouping. Use representative topics without
wording that implies a desired category. Open sorts elicit groups and names;
closed sorts test placement in supplied groups; hybrid sorts combine them.
Inspect ambiguous/unsorted cards and disagreement by audience. Sorting does not
prove that people can find a destination in navigation.

**Tree test** checks a text hierarchy without layout, search, or article content.
Define accepted destinations for each realistic scenario before running the study.
Use task language rather than category labels; choose representative frequent or
consequential tasks. Separate relevant audience groups and vary task order.
Record success, first choice, direct/return paths, wrong destinations, abandonment,
and relevant assistance. Timing is secondary and depends on study conditions.
Ask briefly about uncertainty after a task without suggesting an answer.

Choose success thresholds and sample size for the actual decision before testing.
A pilot identifies candidate problems; it does not estimate reliable population
percentages. A difference between new and experienced users is a finding to
explain, not proof of jargon or overlapping categories. Compare candidate labels
or structures with comparable tasks; account for order effects. Re-test revisions.
Tree success does not establish whole-interface usability or article usefulness.

## Interpret and decide

Separate observations, quotes, interpretations, solutions, and missing evidence.
Keep contradictory cases and unrepresented audiences visible. Pair an intended
success measure with a harm guardrail. If a pattern has several explanations,
choose the follow-up that distinguishes them before making a causal claim.
