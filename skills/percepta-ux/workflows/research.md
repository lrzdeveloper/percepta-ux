# Research

Read [Research methods](../references/research-methods.md) before planning or
interpreting a study. It contains shared evidence rules and a complete
classification/findability protocol. For another method, read its relevant section
in [Research protocols](../references/research-protocols.md). Match the method
to the uncertainty. Reuse supplied findings
that answer the question. Distinguish observations, interpretations, and missing
evidence.

For a plan, define the decision, audience/context, tasks or questions, evidence
to collect, limitations, and the observation that would change the design.
For synthesis, trace conclusions to supplied evidence and note contradictory or
unrepresented cases. A component example is not user research.

For a brief capped plan, select one or two representative tasks and the evidence
that changes the decision. Keep participant definitions provisional unless
supplied; a short plan need not list every procedural detail or example.

Done: deliver an executable plan or evidence-grounded synthesis in the requested
format. Proposed studies remain explicitly unexecuted.
