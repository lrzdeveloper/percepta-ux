# Design

Use the supplied brief, content, audience, constraints, and existing components.
Resolve only material unknowns; existing context is a brief, not a reason to begin
another interview. Preserve identity unless a redesign is requested.

Read [Product context](../references/product-context.md) for a new surface, redesign,
or a material conflict in existing guidance.

Read [Visual craft](../references/visual-craft.md) for hierarchy and direction and
[Components](../references/components.md) for meaningful states. For uncertain
tradeoffs, explain the viable alternatives and recommend one against the goal;
keep routine choices implicit.

Deliver the requested specification, mockup, or implementation. Include material
loading, empty, failure, and recovery behavior. For requested implementation,
complete the scoped artifact and verify relevant behavior rather than stopping
at a proposal.

Done: the requested artifact serves the task, preserves constraints, and has an
honest check record. A narrow change needs only the decisions that affect it.
