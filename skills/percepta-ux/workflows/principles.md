# Principles

Read [Team principles](../references/team-principles.md) before defining rules. Start with recurring
product decisions and existing vocabulary. Connect each priority to a relevant
mechanism, local application, exception, and review criterion.

Keep chosen numeric constraints distinct from scientific findings. Prefer rules
that resolve actual tradeoffs over a catalogue of laws or abstract slogans.

Done: a concise, applicable set of team rules, revisable with product evidence.
