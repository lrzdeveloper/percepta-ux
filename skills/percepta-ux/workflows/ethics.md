# Ethics

Read [Ethics](../references/ethics.md) before inspecting effects on agency, comprehension,
privacy, refusal, and exit. Separate protective effort from obstructive effort.
Identify actual behavior and consequences; keep appearance-based concerns labeled
as hypotheses.

Preserve the legitimate product goal while proposing a concrete user-respecting
alternative where needed. Define useful success and harm signals. Keep legal or
medical conclusions within the available authoritative evidence.

Done: the affected choice and people, material risks, practical alternatives,
and a way to check outcomes are concrete.
